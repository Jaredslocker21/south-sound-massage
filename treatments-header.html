Treatments built around real life

Each treatment is structured around your primary goal: performance, work-life strain, or rehabilitation support. We keep it clinical, clear, and trackable.

Sports Therapy

Performance • Recovery • Tissue quality

Built for training load: heavy legs, tight hips, shoulder restriction, and recurring strain patterns. We focus on output and recovery — not “relaxation-only” work.

  • Post-training recovery & soreness management
  • Hips, calves, hamstrings, shoulders
  • Massage + mobility integration when useful

Work creates repeatable patterns: neck/shoulders, upper back, forearms/hands, jaw, hips and feet. Treatment targets the pattern — then we keep it stable with simple movement rules.

Rehabilitation Therapy

Stiffness • Recovery • Return-to-load
