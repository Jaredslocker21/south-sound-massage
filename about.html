About me

Jared Middle Calf

Certified through Branschrådet Svensk Massage, I’ve worked as a massage therapist for over six years, supporting both private clients and companies across Skåne.

With a background in theatre and event production, industrial work, retail managemnet, restaurants, and many desk-jobs, I understand the physical strain modern work places on the body. Massage changed my own life — and today, my work focuses on helping others reduce pain, improve movement, and recover sustainably. Friskvårdsbidrag accepted.

I’m especially interested in how the nervous system and movement patterns shape pain. That’s why my sessions often mix hands-on treatment with mobility work — helping your body relearn what “normal” feels like.

Outside the treatment room, I value clarity, consistency, and respecting people’s time. In sessions, that means listening closely, working deliberately, and never doing more than your body can actually integrate.

The South Sound Method

Simple, structured, and tailored to your body — not a template.

1) Listen

Quick check-in: what changed since last time, what matters today, what your week demands.

2) Release

Hands-on work to reduce pain and normalize tissue: Swedish classic, deep tissue, trigger points, fascia.

3) Restore

Range-of-motion work with PNF / PIR and stretching—so the change actually “holds.”

4) Repeat

We track what improves and adjust. Less guessing. More progress.

FAQ

Do I need to know which treatment to book?

Nope. Book the time you want. We decide the style after a quick check-in.

Is it always deep tissue?

Not at all. Sometimes deep work is perfect — other times it fights your nervous system. I choose the level that creates change without leaving you wrecked.

What should I wear?

Wear what’s comfortable. For sports-focused sessions, flexible clothing can help if we do movement work. Otherwise, we’ll adapt.

How fast will I notice results?

Many clients feel a difference after session one. Long-term patterns usually respond best with a short series (weekly or every other week) then maintenance.
